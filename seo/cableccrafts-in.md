## How to paste this into Shopify

For each collection or page: **Search engine listing → Edit**. Put the **Title** in the page title field and the **Meta description** in the description field. These titles already include `| Cablec Crafts`. Do not let the theme add the shop name a second time.

Put the **H1** in the collection title or the page heading. Put the **Intro** in the collection description or the page body, above the product grid.

Titles are about 45-60 characters. Descriptions are about 140-160 characters. The privacy and terms titles are shorter on purpose. Those pages should not compete for product searches.
